How to use this desk

  1. Wait for a call. Most rounds say No bet. That's normal: Kalshi's prices are usually fair.
  2. When it says Buy Up or Buy Down, place that bet in Kalshi for the amount shown under Bet.
  3. Log it here. Results fill in by themselves. After 30–50 bets you'll know if the edge is real.

Is the price right?

Kalshi's price is the market's guess at the chance. Our chance comes from how far BTC is from the target and how fast it's moving. A bet is good value only when our chance beats the price plus fee by at least your minimum advantage.

Why: the evidence

Each signal shows what is pushing BTC right now. Distance to the target and time left decide most 15-minute rounds; the other signals show short-term pressure and can flip quickly. They explain the move, they don't guarantee it.

          Kalshi's rules and our data sources

          Loading Kalshi's rules…

          Kalshi settles on CF Benchmarks' BRTI, which isn't public in real time. This desk estimates it as Coinbase plus the gap measured from Kalshi's recent targets, and adds that uncertainty to the odds. Our chances are model estimates from recent price movement, not guarantees. Size every bet so a loss doesn't matter.
