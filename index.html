      How this market settles

      Loading Kalshi's rules…

      Kalshi settles on CF Benchmarks' BRTI, which isn't public in real time. This desk estimates it as Coinbase plus the gap measured from Kalshi's recent targets, and adds that uncertainty to the odds. Odds are model estimates from recent price movement, not guarantees. Size every bet so a loss doesn't matter.
