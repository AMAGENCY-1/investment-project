    How the projections work

    Method, rules and limits
    • Minutes: a blend of the last 5 games, this season and last season.
    • Production per minute: this season, leaning on last season until about 10 games are played, plus recent form.
    • Opponent: scoring props are nudged by how many points the opponent allows.
    • Chance of hitting the line: from the player's own game-to-game swings, with extra room for uncertainty.
    • Suggestions only show when the advantage clears your minimum, the market isn't too thin (spread of 10¢ or less), the player isn't on the injury report, and the game hasn't started.

    Props are high-variance: one game is a small sample, and Kalshi's prices already include most public stats. Treat suggestions as a short list to check, not a sure thing.
